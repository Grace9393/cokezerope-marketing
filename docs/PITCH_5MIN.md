# 5-minute finalist pitch: CokeZerope Marketing

**0:00–0:40 · The pain (one person, one Monday)**

- A make-up category or marketing manager in Taiwan, every Monday: why did sales move, and what do we do about competitors on momo, Sephora and Watsons, and about what Dcard and Instagram are saying?
- Today: spreadsheets, ten browser tabs, screenshots in LINE groups. The dashboard headline reads "AURUM GOLD −55 %", and that's wrong: 2026 only has half a year of data.

**0:40–1:00 · The idea**

- Ace is an agent that does the analysis and proposes actions. It never acts on its own: every action needs a human Approve.
- Its edge: it checks whether the numbers are comparable before it explains them, and it cites every number.
- One switch, the demo mask, re-skins every brand as Cola, Pika or K-pop, so teams can share and present safely.

**1:00–3:00 · Live demo (run against pre-filled pages)**

1. **Category view:** open Ace. Show the plan, then the tool calls (like-for-like query, live momo scrape, price gap), then the brief: **+5.1 % like-for-like, not −55 %**, driven by Cushion Foundation +243 % while Silk Veil fell 46 %. Click a citation and the row highlights.
2. **Pulse:** flip the mask from Pika to K-pop live. Show the social scan of three rival cushions (share of voice, sentiment, topics, search-grounded posts) and Ace's brief, which links our Cushion growth to rival buzz and live momo promotions.
3. **Voice:** Gradium reads the brief aloud, and we ask a follow-up by push-to-talk.
4. **Human in control:** Approve "Add to watchlist", Edit and Approve the team email (mailto draft only), then show the Activity log.

**3:00–4:00 · How it works (one slide: the README Mermaid diagram)**

- **Gemini Interactions API:**
  - Flash-Lite router and plan
  - 3.8 Flash function-calling loop, with server-side state through `previous_interaction_id`
  - structured brief
  - Google Search grounding for social listening
  - URL context for retailers
  - the **Deep Research managed agent** in the background
  - Nano Banana for promo drafts
- **Gradium:** TTS and ASR for EN/DE/FR, with a brand-name normalisation pass. Gemini covers Mandarin.
- **Entire:** agent sessions captured alongside the commits.
- **Guardrails:**
  - evidence ids validated against tool output
  - untrusted framing for anything from the web
  - budgets and rate limits
  - signed job handles
  - no server-side write path
  - synthetic demo data plus a display-only brand mask

**4:00–4:40 · Why it matters / what's next**

- From hours to about 2 minutes, with every number cited and every action approved.
- Next: scheduled scans with alerts on negative-sentiment spikes, official platform APIs, team-shared watchlists, and more categories and markets.

**4:40–5:00 · Close**

- "Ace doesn't just answer. It checks, cites, and asks before it acts."

**Likely Q&A**

- _Hallucinations?_ Numbers come only from tools, and citations are validated server-side. Social links are labelled search-grounded or unverified.
- _Why not let it send the email?_ Irreversible actions need a human. Ace drafts, the human approves, and the draft opens in the mail client.
- _Is the data real?_ The demo sales data is synthetic, generated by `tools/synthetic-data/generate.py`. Competitor prices and social posts are live public web data.
- _Latency?_ Router about 3 s; analysis 40–120 s depending on live scraping. Deep Research runs in the background.
